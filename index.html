How social media actually works

Reach
Platforms use a ranking algorithm that decides who sees your content, based on signals like watch time, replies, and shares — not just follower count.
Signals
Early engagement (the first minutes/hours after posting) tells the algorithm whether to push a post further. This is why timing and a strong hook matter.
Consistency
Accounts that post on a regular rhythm and stay inside one niche tend to be shown to more of the right audience over time.
Trust
Verified info, two-factor login, and clean posting history all feed into how much a platform trusts an account — which affects reach and account safety.
Community
Replies, DMs, and shares turn casual viewers into a real audience — growth is a relationship, not just a number.

Cyber security basics we always share

Turn on 2FA

Two-factor authentication is the single biggest thing that stops account takeovers.

Watch for phishing

Fake login pages and "urgent account issue" messages are the most common way accounts get stolen.

Unique passwords

Never reuse the same password across platforms — use a password manager if you can.

Check app permissions

Regularly review which third-party apps have access to your social accounts and revoke what you don't use.

Verify before you click

Hover or long-press links before opening — shortened links can hide malicious destinations.

Back up recovery info

Keep your recovery email/phone current so you can always get back into a locked account.
